# How to Play the Card Pair Matching Game

## Objective
Match all the pairs of cards in as few moves as possible.

## Instructions

1. Click "Start" to begin the game.
2. Choose the number of pairs (10, 15, or 20).
3. Select a category for the cards (Fruits , Vegetables).
4. Click on two cards to reveal them.
   - If they match, they will disappear.
   - If not, they will flip back.
5. The game ends when all pairs are matched.

## Tips
- Try to remember the position of each card.
